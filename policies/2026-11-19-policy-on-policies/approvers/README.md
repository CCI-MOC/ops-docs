This is a supporting document to the [Policy on Policies](../). It records who
currently holds the roles that policy defines, and which teams are currently
available to be named as a policy's `responsible-team`.

It is supporting content. It records how the policy's requirements are
currently satisfied; it does not create, relax, or contradict any requirement.
Changing a name in the tables below does not change the rules in the policy.
Changing the rules — for example, the requirement that all three institutions
approve — is an amendment to the policy itself.

## Designated policy approvers

Each institution designates one approver for policy pull requests, and one
alternate who records that institution's approval when the designated approver
is the author of the pull request. These roles are per institution, not per
policy: the same people approve every policy pull request in this repository.

## Responsible teams

Every policy names a `responsible-team` in its frontmatter: the team
accountable for keeping that policy accurate, for maintaining the supporting
documents it points to, and for answering questions about how it is applied.
The policy requires that value to be one of the teams listed below.

| Team | Accountable for | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MOC Governance | Policy and governance for the MOC environment. | TBD |
| MOC Operations | Day-to-day operation of the MOC environment. | TBD |

Adding a team to this table does not give it authority over normative
content. Its authority extends to supporting content only.

## Maintenance

The responsible team maintains this document and may update it without the
full approval process, subject to the restriction on approver entries above.

This document follows the convention the policy sets for supporting documents
kept in a policy's directory: it lives in its own subdirectory as
`approvers/README.md`, so that the link `approvers/` resolves both on GitHub
and on the published site, and it sets `eleventyExcludeFromCollections: true`
so that it is published as a page without appearing in the policies index as a
policy in its own right.
